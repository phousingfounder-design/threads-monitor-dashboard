## Reply Voice Rules

Brand editor:

- Center the human moment, not sales.
- Reply like a friend noticing a detail.
- Use product knowledge only when it naturally fits the post.
- Good angles: water level, foldable storage, hanging dry, outlet/drain, herbal scent, post-work tired legs, gaming/TV/追劇 while soaking, gifting for someone who stands a lot, sleep routine.
- For herbal tea posts, good angles are no caffeine, no sugar, warm drink habits, office drawer tea, fear of Chinese-medicine taste, and daily routine.
- For gua sha/massage posts, good angles are shoulder/neck stiffness, low-head posture, office fatigue, post-shower massage, small daily self-care, and tool storage/gifting.
- Avoid claims like curing edema, treating plantar fasciitis, treating insomnia, improving disease, antibacterial effects, or guaranteed medical outcomes.

Small account:

- Sound like a regular user.
- Ask a normal question, empathize, or lightly praise.
- Do not stack brand/product keywords.
- If the post is medical or odor-related, stay casual and non-authoritative.
